The World

This is a medieval fantasy world on one enormous continent. Technology is high-medieval. Five major kingdoms are separated by vast distances, while much of the continent remains unsettled because monsters are a constant threat.

Travel between kingdoms is a serious undertaking. Roads and established routes exist, but travelers may need preparation, a safe route, or hired protection. The wilderness between settlements is not merely empty land—it is a dangerous part of the world in its own right.

The Five Kingdoms

Human Kingdom — Northwest

Mainly human, with a small demi-human population. It is generally unfriendly toward other kingdoms and species, though it maintains trade. Religion is strongest here. The church is its own institution and does not control most government decisions. Religion is completely separate from magic.

Elven Kingdom — Northeast

An insular elven realm. Many elves consider themselves superior to other peoples, though they are not generally hostile. Elves have exceptional eyesight, are naturally excellent archers, and live around 600 years. Some leave to pursue relationships with humans, while many consider this foolish because humans have much shorter lifespans.

Demi-Human Kingdom — Southwest

Roughly 70–80% demi-human, with most of the remainder human. It is not especially wealthy or powerful but has extensive land and resources. It is primarily agricultural and exports large quantities of food. Because of its relative weakness, it tries to remain friendly with everyone. Its demi-human population is large and rapidly growing.

Half-Dragon Kingdom — Southeast Mountains

An isolated kingdom built among vast mountain ranges and inhabited entirely by half-dragons. Outsiders are normally forbidden entry except for diplomacy and major trades. It is highly self-sufficient and mainly exports excess metals and gems to the Demi-Human and Open Trade Kingdoms in exchange for goods.

Open Trade Kingdom — Center

Located near the center of the continent, this is the main crossroads between the other kingdoms. Its population is roughly 60% human, 20% half-elf, and 20% other species. It is highly accepting and a major center of commerce—a heaven for entrepreneurs and adventurers. It has many adventurers' guilds and inns and is a natural starting point for adventurers.

Species

Humans

Humans are the baseline species.

Demi-Humans

Demi-human technically refers to any human hybrid, including half-dragons, but normally means half-human, half-animal people. Their animal traits are limited to furred ears and tails; otherwise they are human in appearance and biology. Their ears provide enhanced hearing and are sensitive. They are generally similar to humans in height and strength. Known types include fox, wolf, dog, cat, and half-elf demi-humans. Dog demi-humans inherit ear and tail traits from their breed.

Half-Dragons

Half-dragons are technically human hybrids but are never called demi-humans and are considered their own people. Most are 190–210 cm tall; 180 cm is short and 220 cm is tall. They are stronger than humans and have powerful senses. Their long, pointed ears angle slightly downward. Scales appear only below the knees, on the feet, forearms, hands, and tail. Scales and horns can be emerald green, crimson red, or amethyst purple; pearly white is extremely rare. Their horns curve upward and backward, are extremely durable and valuable, and reach roughly 12–16 inches. They live around 400 years.

Elves

Elves are a separate species, known for exceptional eyesight and natural skill with bows. They live around 600 years.

Monsters & Wilderness

Monsters are extremely common outside civilization. Near towns and in ordinary forests are threats such as direwolves, slimes, and werewolves. Deeper forests, caves, dungeons, and mountains contain stronger creatures such as large orcs, goblin hordes, and dragons.

Monsters occasionally band together and attack settlements, with attacks occurring somewhere in the world roughly every few weeks. Anyone traveling outside civilization must be able to defend themselves, follow a safe route, or hire protection.

Magic

Magic is based on mana. Everyone is born with a maximum mana capacity determined largely by genetics. Increasing it requires extensive training beginning at a very young age; by adulthood, it is fixed. Fewer than 1% of people know enough magic to use more than a few basic spells.

The Three Schools

  • Arcane: manipulation of raw magical energy.
  • Elemental: manipulation of natural elements and forces.
  • Vital: manipulation of living biological systems, allowing healing, accelerated recovery, treatment of illness and poisons, regeneration, and other biological effects.

Specific elements, spells, and techniques are left open for individual users to create.

Newly learned spells have a fixed form and mana cost. Skilled users can modify familiar spells by putting more or less mana into them, changing their power. Powerful spells require long incantations and intense concentration, leaving casters vulnerable. Battle mages therefore usually fight from protected positions or within adventuring parties. Skilled magic users are especially valuable as doctors because of Vital Magic.

Mana naturally regenerates from the air. Completely exhausting one's mana causes unconsciousness, followed by severe fatigue. Mana also subconsciously sustains the body with nutrients, so people with large mana pools can go unusually long without food or water. Exhausting their mana can cause sudden intense hunger.

Warriors & Mana Reinforcement

Mana also benefits warriors. Persistent combat or training causes the body to gradually reinforce itself with mana over months or years. This is subconscious and cannot simply be taught; it develops through repeated physical stress and must be trained like a muscle.

It allows experienced warriors to grow stronger beyond their apparent natural physical peak, while improving physical performance, endurance, and recovery. Minor injuries and muscle tears may heal roughly two or three times faster, allowing more frequent training and faster physical development. Half-dragons are particularly proficient at this reinforcement.

There Is No Predetermined Path

There is no chosen hero, prophecy, or required storyline. The world simply exists, and its people live, work, fight, explore, trade, and pursue their own ambitions. You can become an adventurer, merchant, warrior, mage, craftsman, traveler, or something entirely different.

The world responds to what you choose to do.
